Who does what

    Anti-nuke

    twelve guards, each with its own switch

    The bot reads the audit log the moment Discord pushes an entry, so it knows who did it before the damage finishes. Everything the attacker did is reverted. Then the punishment lands.

    Preventive
    Punish on the first action. ,setup turns this on.
    Reactive
    Punish after 3 actions within 15 seconds. Switch with ,antinukemode reactive.
    Instant guards
    Channel delete, role delete, bot add and webhook create punish on the first action in both modes.
    Punishment
    ban, kick, timeout for one day, or strip to remove every dangerous role. Pick with ,punishment.
    Trusted people
    The server owner, extra owners and anyone you whitelist with ,wl add are never punished.

    Features

    the rest of the server, covered

    • Automod

      Link, mention and blocked-word filters, plus an anti-spam that deletes the spam and times the member out for 10 minutes. Anti-spam is not turned on for you: you switch it on with ,antispam on. Blocked messages go to the mod log.

    • Moderation

      Ban, hardban, softban, kick, timeout and jail, with purge, lock, unlock and slowmode. Role hierarchy is checked, so nobody can act on someone above them.

    • Logs

      Separate channels for messages, voice, moderation, members and channel or role changes. Every entry shows a profile block, and channel, role and ban events show who did it. ,autosetuplogs creates them all.

    • Tickets

      A panel with Open, Close and Claim buttons. Transcripts go to the log channel and to the ticket owner by direct message when the ticket closes.

    • Join to create

      Members join a creator channel and get their own temporary voice room.

    • Verification and welcome

      Button verification that hands out a role, plus welcome and farewell messages you can edit as embeds.

    • Leveling

      XP from chatting, rank cards, a top 10 leaderboard and role rewards at the levels you choose.

    • Message level

      Counts every message and gives a role at each milestone you pick: 500 messages, 1000 messages, and so on. Milestones have their own announcement channel.

    • Copy server

      Run ,copyserver to copy roles, channels and settings into a code, then ,pasteserver <code> in another server to add them or to clear and rebuild it.

    • Mention the bot

      Mention it on its own and it replies with its features, its owner and how many servers and users it protects. Every command also replies to the message that ran it.

    Setup

    six steps, in this order

    1. 1

      Invite the bot

      It asks for Administrator so it can revert changes and ban on its own. Use the invite link, which includes slash commands.

    2. 2

      Move its role to the top

      Open Server Settings, then Roles, and drag the bot's role above everyone else. It cannot act on anyone above it, and Discord rejects a ban from a lower role.

    3. 3

      Run ,setup

      Turns on anti-nuke (ban on the first action) and automod, and creates the log channels. Anti-spam stays off until you run ,antispam on.

    4. 4

      Whitelist your staff

      Run ,wl add @staff. Anyone not whitelisted who deletes channels, bans members or similar gets punished.

    5. 5

      Add tickets and voice rooms

      Run ,ticket setup for the ticket panel and ,j2c setup for join-to-create voice rooms. ,setup does not do these two.

    6. 6

      Look around

      Run ,help to browse every command. Use ,help ban for the usage and aliases of a single one, or mention the bot to see what it can do.
